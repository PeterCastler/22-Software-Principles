# Source note: KISS Software Design Principle

- Source: Baeldung on Computer Science
- URL: https://www.baeldung.com/cs/kiss-software-design-principle
- Accessed: 2026-07-15
- Type: secondary explanation

The article defines KISS as preferring simplicity over avoidable complexity. It connects simple programs with easier reading, maintenance, debugging, and testing, and recommends small, transparent modules plus removal of unused elements.

For this library, the important qualification is **adequate simplicity**: preserve behavior and quality constraints while minimizing mechanisms. KISS is not permission to omit error handling, security, or required tests.
