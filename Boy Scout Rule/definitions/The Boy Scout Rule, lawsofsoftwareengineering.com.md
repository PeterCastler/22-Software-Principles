# Source note: The Boy Scout Rule

- Author: Milan Milanović
- URL: https://lawsofsoftwareengineering.com/laws/boy-scout-rule/
- Accessed: 2026-07-15
- Type: practical synthesis

The Boy Scout Rule adapts “leave the campsite cleaner than you found it” to code: make small continuous improvements in the area being changed. Examples include clearer naming, simpler logic, and removing nearby cruft.

The crucial constraint for agentic work is scope. Cleanup should be related, reviewable, behavior-preserving, and backed by proportionate verification—not an invitation to roam the repository or bundle a redesign into an unrelated task.
