# Source note: Architectural Principles — Separation of Concerns

- Publisher: Microsoft Learn
- URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/architecture/modern-web-apps-azure/architectural-principles#separation-of-concerns
- Accessed: 2026-07-15
- Type: architectural guidance

Separation of Concerns organizes software by distinct kinds of work. The source distinguishes selecting noteworthy items from formatting them, and more broadly separates business rules from infrastructure and UI. The purpose is independent reasoning, testing, and evolution—not maximizing the number of layers.

The principle follows Dijkstra's earlier use of “separation of concerns” as a way to focus attention on different aspects of a problem, even when a complete system must eventually combine them.
