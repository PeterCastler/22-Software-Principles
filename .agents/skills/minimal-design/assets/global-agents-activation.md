## Software decision filtration

- For every request to create, modify, debug, refactor, delete, migrate, review, or architect software, invoke `$minimal-design` before implementation or review.
- Let the skill fast-exit genuinely mechanical work; do not force a brief or principle onto every task.
- Re-enter the skill when implementation reveals a new contract, consumer, irreversible effect, or unresolved product choice.
- Pause only at its Decision Gate for the smallest product decision the repository cannot answer; keep engineering choices with the agent.
- At closeout, review the diff and verification evidence, promote only stable knowledge future developers must know, and discard temporary reasoning scaffolding.
