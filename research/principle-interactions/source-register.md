## Interaction-research sources

Interaction-specific sources are added here during Phase 4. A profile source may later support a pair only when its text actually establishes the claimed mechanism; mere presence in this table is not interaction evidence.

| ID | Source | Source type | Interaction claims supported | Limits and use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I001 | [Is Design Dead? — Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/articles/designDead.html) | Authoritative synthesis of XP evolutionary design | Simple design and YAGNI are explicitly connected; removal of duplication is one simple-design criterion; refactoring supplies the malleability needed to defer speculative design | Direct only for the interactions the article actually names; it does not make every simplicity principle interchangeable |
| I002 | [Continuous Design — Martin Fowler](https://www.martinfowler.com/ieeeSoftware/continuousDesign.pdf) | Published IEEE Software article | Present-day, simple, cohesive, decoupled, isolated, and nonduplicative design rules are used together, while speculative hooks and premature generality are rejected | Supports convergent interaction among the operational rules; it is not empirical proof that every decomposition reduces code |
| I003 | [Beck Design Rules — Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BeckDesignRules.html) | Authoritative synthesis | No duplication and fewest elements are explicit rules; removal must remain subordinate to expressing intention and preserving behavior | Direct evidence for DRY/simplicity interaction and its readability boundary |
| I004 | [The Rule of Three — Nicolas Carlo](https://understandlegacycode.com/blog/refactoring-rule-of-three/) | Detailed practitioner analysis with worked examples | Explicitly moderates DRY: a wrong abstraction can cost more than duplication, so wait for repeated evidence | Direct interaction discussion, but the exact number three remains a heuristic rather than an experimentally established threshold |
| I005 | [XML Schema 1.1 Part 1: Structures — W3C](https://www.w3.org/TR/xmlschema11-1/#intro-purpose) | W3C Recommendation | Applies the Rule of Least Power to the choice between declarative identity/type mechanisms and general assertions | Concrete domain example; does not establish that declarative representation is always clearer or cheaper |
| I006 | [Re: Rule of Least Power — W3C TAG mailing list](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-tag/2006Feb/0075.html) | Contemporary clarification by W3C TAG participants | Clarifies that least power is justified by useful analysis and should not force awkward use of an inadequate declarative language | Boundary evidence for Least Power interactions |
| I007 | [Parse, don't validate — Alexis King](https://lexi-lambda.github.io/blog/2019/11/05/parse-don-t-validate/) | Originating essay with worked code | Explicitly connects parsing to making illegal states unrepresentable; shows duplicated representations creating illegal states; separates parsing from execution and identifies shotgun parsing | Direct for named links; examples are predominantly statically typed functional code |

| I010 | [DIP in the Wild — Brett Schuchert](https://martinfowler.com/articles/dipInTheWild.html) | Production case study and synthesis | Explicitly applies YAGNI and SRP while discovering domain-owned abstractions; shows the cost of flexible abstractions, narrowed interfaces, composition, and avoiding irrelevant library surface | Direct for interactions explicitly discussed; other deductions are graded Convergent or Reasoned Inference |
| I011 | [The Composition Over Inheritance Principle — Brandon Rhodes](https://python-patterns.guide/gang-of-four/composition-over-inheritance/) | Detailed worked pattern analysis | Demonstrates subclass explosion, adapters, bridges, decorators, delegation, and reuse of standard adapters | Supports mechanisms involving variation axes and adapter cost; not a universal ban on inheritance |
