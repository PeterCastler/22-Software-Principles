# Occam's Razor ↔ Principle of Least Power

Assessment status: Final publish; validated for freeze  
Primary classification: Reinforcement  
Secondary classifications: None  
Evidence grade: Convergent  
Confidence: high

## Shared decision surface

Causally connected decisions: Occam's Razor governs selection among equally adequate designs or explanations; Principle of Least Power governs expressive power of the chosen mechanism.

## Interaction mechanism

Among adequate mechanisms, the less expressive option commonly introduces fewer unsupported behaviors and assumptions.

## Material consequence

A constrained data or query mechanism wins when arbitrary code supplies no required capability.

## Context in which it applies

Applies when candidate mechanisms express the same complete requirement.

## Counterexample or boundary

A weaker language requiring a hidden custom interpreter introduces more entities and is not the parsimonious choice.
