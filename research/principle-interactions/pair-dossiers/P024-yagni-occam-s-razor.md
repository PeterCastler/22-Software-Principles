## Shared decision surface

Causally connected decisions: YAGNI governs timing of capability and extensibility; Occam's Razor governs selection among equally adequate designs or explanations.

## Interaction mechanism

Presumptive capability rests on unsupported future assumptions that Occam's comparison asks designers not to multiply.

## Material consequence

An architecture option loses when its extra components are justified only by forecast consumers rather than current evidence.

## Context in which it applies

Applies when otherwise adequate designs differ mainly in assumed future needs.
