# YAGNI ↔ DRY

Assessment status: Final publish; validated for freeze  
Primary classification: Moderation  
Secondary classifications: None  
Evidence grade: Convergent  
Confidence: high

## Shared decision surface

Causally connected decisions: YAGNI governs timing of capability and extensibility; DRY governs authority for changing knowledge.

## Interaction mechanism

YAGNI blocks abstractions built for predicted reuse, while DRY still requires one authority for duplicated knowledge that already changes together.

## Material consequence

The team distinguishes present duplicated authority from hypothetical future similarity before extracting.

## Context in which it applies

Applies when deduplication is proposed before shared ownership or a current second consumer is established.

## Counterexample or boundary
