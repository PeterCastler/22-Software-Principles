# Rule of Three ↔ Convention over Configuration

Assessment status: Final publish; validated for freeze  
Primary classification: Sequencing  
Secondary classifications: None  
Evidence grade: Convergent  
Confidence: high

## Shared decision surface

Causally connected decisions: Rule of Three governs timing and scope of abstraction; Convention over Configuration governs routine defaults versus explicit exceptions.

## Interaction mechanism

Observed repeated choices supply evidence for selecting a dominant convention; designing the convention before examples predicts the wrong default and exceptions.

## Material consequence

Teams inventory real variants first, then standardize the common case and retain narrow overrides.

## Context in which it applies

Applies when a local convention is being introduced from too few real cases.

## Counterexample or boundary

An ecosystem framework convention can be adopted immediately because evidence exists outside the repository.
