# Review and Reconciliation

## Reconciliation protocol

The two profile-only screens were kept blind until both 231-row ledgers existed. Their disagreements formed research obligations, not votes. Targeted sources and counterexamples then produced a proposed ledger. The original independent reviewer subsequently challenged every proposed publication. Final decisions below follow the evidence mechanism; neither screen has automatic priority.

A final `publish` requires the first three gates to pass and Direct, Convergent, or independently accepted high-confidence Reasoned Inference. `Unsupported` always means `reject`. No bundle hypothesis was available during any decision recorded here.
